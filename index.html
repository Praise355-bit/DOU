The Room

Velvet seating, low gold light, and tables dressed for the occasion — whatever the occasion turns out to be.

A room built for long dinners.

We opened Dou to hold two kitchens at once — the continental training of our chefs and the flavours we grew up on — without asking either to sit quietly.

Low light, warm wood, a lounge that runs late. Come for the steak, stay for the second round.
